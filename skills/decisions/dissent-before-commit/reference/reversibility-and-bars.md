# Reversibility, and the bar it sets

Classify before canvassing anything. Doing it afterwards invites classifying the
door to suit the objection you got.

| Class | Meaning | Bar for proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| **one-way door** | Not reversible without significant cost. Once it fires the team owns the outcome | Dissent must be **addressed in the action**. An accepted tradeoff is not sufficient |
| **two-way door** | Reversible at modest cost within a reasonable time | Addressed **or** accepted with a named tradeoff |
| **partial reversal** | Reversible at material cost, or only partly | Addressed preferred; an accepted tradeoff requires a named owner of the reversal cost |

The asymmetry is the point. Raising rigor on an irreversible action is obvious;
removing the accept path entirely is what stops a warmly acknowledged objection
from clearing a one-way door.
