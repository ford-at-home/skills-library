# Component bands and channel targets

Five components, 0–20 each. Lower is better. Sum to 100.

| Component | 0 | 20 |
|---|---|---|
| **Buried Catalyst** | Ask in the first ~20%, visually distinct | Ask in the final ~20%, or absent |
| **Scent Trail** | Header, bolded anchors, bullets, whitespace; skimmable in five seconds | Unbroken wall; every word must be read to find the point |
| **Metabolic Density** | Average ≤18 words per sentence, few nested clauses, earned jargon | Average ≥28 words, stacked clauses, unexplained jargon |
| **Handoff Clarity** | Explicit: "no action needed", "approve or reject by Friday" | No closing cue at all |
| **Channel Fit** | Inside the target range; register matches | Severe mismatch: 800 words in chat, two sentences as a strategy memo |

Bands within each component:

- **0–4** ideal, **5–9** minor, **10–14** noticeable, **15–20** broken.
- Buried Catalyst by position: first 20% → 0–4; 20–50% → 5–9; 50–80% → 10–14;
  final 20% or absent → 15–20.
- Metabolic Density by measured average: ≤18 → 0–4; 18–22 → 5–9; 22–28 → 10–14;
  ≥28 → 15–20. Compute it; do not estimate it.
- Channel Fit by distance from target: inside → 0–4; up to 50% over → 5–9;
  50–150% over → 10–14; beyond that, or under half the floor → 15–20.

## Channel targets

| Channel | Target | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Chat DM | ≤80 words | 1–3 sentences, or 3–5 bullets |
| Chat channel post | ≤200 words | Pulse header, bullets, handoff |
| Internal email | 100–400 words | Subject line is the Pulse; body is reasoning plus ask |
| Shared doc | 400–2000 words | Headings, links to raw data |
| Meeting agenda | ≤150 words | Bullets, time budget, decisions needed |
| Strategy memo | 600–1500 words | Structured argument, layered depth |

Too short fails as hard as too long. A two-sentence message carrying a decision
forces every reader to ask for the context individually.
