# Session: Imagine the future

**Length:** 90 minutes (a 60-minute version is at the end). **Size:** 5–9 people. **Facilitator:** the driver, or a neutral colleague. **Materials:** the plan, printed or shared; a shared board; sticky notes or a shared doc.

## Ground rules (read aloud, one minute)

- The goal is the anchor. The plan is a draft.
- Disagreement is the point. Argue about the trade-offs, not the people.
- Decisions belong to whoever holds the authority for them. Everyone else advises.
- The driver keeps the decision log and owns the follow-up.
- Recording is off unless everyone agrees at the start.

## Agenda

### 0:00–0:10 · Frame the goal

- The driver reads the goal statement aloud. Nothing else from the plan yet.
- Each person says, in one sentence: "I would believe we got there if I saw ___."
- The driver notes any indicator people mention that the plan is missing.

### 0:10–0:30 · Future-back: it worked

- "It is <horizon date>. The goal happened, and it stuck."
- **Five minutes of silent writing.** Each person writes:
  - What is different in the layer they know best.
  - What had to be true in the layer below it for that to happen.
- **Share from the slowest layer to the fastest.** Governance, culture, and capacity voices go first; they set the constraints the faster layers must work within.
- The driver maps each contribution to a layer and notes conditions the plan does not yet create.

### 0:30–0:50 · Pre-mortem: it failed

- "It is <horizon date>. The goal did not happen, or it happened and then slipped back."
- **Five minutes of silent writing.** Each person writes the most likely reason, and tags it with a layer or a boundary between two layers.
- Read them out. Cluster by layer and boundary.
- Vote: pick the three most likely failure causes.
- Check: does the plan already address them? If not, mark them for the next segment.

### 0:50–1:10 · Walk the plan by pace

- Go through Now, Next, and Commit moves in order.
- For each group, ask:
  - Does what we imagined in the future-back require this move?
  - Does it address one of the top three failure causes?
  - Is the owner in the room and willing?
  - Is it a one-way or two-way door?
- Add, cut, or re-sequence moves. The driver records changes live.

### 1:10–1:25 · Decide

- Take the decision list in order. For each decision:
  - The person with authority decides now, or
  - Names the date and the evidence they need to decide.
- Always settle these before leaving:
  - Who owns each boundary the plan crosses.
  - How leaders will respond to the first visible failure caused by this change.
  - What the plan stops doing to make room.

### 1:25–1:30 · Commit

- Each person names one thing they will do before the next convening, and by when.
- The driver confirms the working cadence and the date of the next session.

## After the session (driver, same day)

- Update the plan: moves, owners, decisions, and the three failure causes.
- Send the decision log to the room and the review list.
- Note what surprised you. Surprises are where the model of the organization was wrong.

## 60-minute version

| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Frame the goal |
| 0:05–0:20 | Future-back (three minutes of writing; share slowest first) |
| 0:20–0:35 | Pre-mortem (three minutes of writing; pick the top two) |
| 0:35–0:50 | Decide (boundary owners, failure response, what stops) |
| 0:50–0:60 | Walk the Now moves and commit |

Next and Commit moves go to the following session.
