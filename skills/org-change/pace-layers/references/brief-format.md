# Working brief format

One file per goal: `pace-layers/<goal-slug>/brief.md`. Each skill appends or updates its own section. Keep entries short and factual. Record the user's words where they matter, and mark every inference as a hypothesis.

```markdown
# Brief: <working title>

Started: <date> · Last updated: <date> · Depth: quick | full | one layer
Driver: <who is running this, usually the user>

## 1. Situation
- What is happening: ...
- Where the user sits: role, scope, decides alone vs. needs others
- Tried before: what, and what happened
- Others' view of the problem: ...
- Deadline or forcing event: ...
- Frontier: AI is a cause | a proposed fix | a bystander
- Initial theory of change (user's words): ...

### Symptom map
| Symptom | Surface layer | Suspected lever layer (hypothesis) |
|---|---|---|

## 2. Goal
(written by goal-at-layer)

## 3. Layer briefs
### <Layer name>
(written by each layer-* skill)

## 4. Shears
(written by shear-check)

## 5. Theory of change and insights
- Theory (restated): ...
- Heuristic tests: H# — holds | bends | breaks — evidence
- Insights (ranked):
  1. Observation / Mechanism / Implication
- Second-order effects: ...

## Unknowns and who would know
| Unknown | Why it matters | Who would know |
|---|---|---|

## Decisions surfaced so far
| Decision | Who has authority | Reversible? |
|---|---|---|
```

Keep the brief free of credentials, customer data, and personal details the plan does not need.
